The long-run mean still has the lowest RMSE at every horizon. The direct forecast is not distinguishable from HAR at any lead. At 60 days its RMSE is below HAR, EWMA, and GARCH, and the gap against the mean is not significant at 5%. It beats one-day EWMA and GARCH. The mean error is +0.102, +0.107, +0.101, and +0.152 at leads 1, 5, 20, and 60. The simulated forecast's errors on those same leads were +0.103, +0.298, +0.650, and +0.929, and that gap grew with the horizon. EWMA's mean error still goes from +1.519 at one day to +0.001 at 60 days, because a one-day EWMA or GARCH number is the log of a variance and the target is the log of a squared return.

Every regression draw met the radius constraint. The second-half complete-data log likelihood on that refit has mean -2560 and standard deviation 38.

## Where this still loses

The direct regression removes the horizon-growing bias of the simulated path. What remains is smaller. Daily `log(r^2)` is still a noisy series: the one-day RMSE is about 2.6 to 2.7, and the posterior self-stay settles near 0.86 to 0.90 rather than the prior mean of 0.98, so a typical run is days to a few weeks. On bitcoin the direct model has the best point forecast at 20 and 60 days, the win over GARCH is clear, and the win over HAR is not significant at 5%. On the S&P 500 the long-run mean wins every horizon, and the direct model is not separable from HAR. A positive mean error of about 0.10 to 0.22 on bitcoin and about 0.10 on the S&P 500 is still there after the training-window correction.

A separate check, with one HAR fit on lags 5, 22, and 66 before the holdout and an EWMA bias correction fit on that same training span, matches the rolling-origin note supplied with the data: Bitcoin lead 20 is EWMA 0.845, HAR 0.762, mean 0.910, and lead 60 is 0.789, 0.684, 0.792. S&P 500 lead 20 is EWMA 0.562, HAR 0.522, mean 0.481, and lead 60 is 0.451, 0.375, 0.310. The holdout tables are the stricter protocol: HAR(1/5/22) refit at each origin, and EWMA and GARCH with no additive correction.

An earlier single-origin sticky HDP-SLDS, scored on overlapping realized volatility rather than this log-mean target, is not part of this package. On that score the SLDS won Bitcoin leads 20 and 60, and EWMA won the longer S&P leads.
